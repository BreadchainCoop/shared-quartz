# BASICS

---

## About

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Jq18HPDX-o](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Jq18HPDX-o)

LaborDAO supports workers who want to organize, strike and collectively bargain in web3 and out through research and funding.

  

## Purpose

Research DAO Labor practices and improve the Web3 space by building powerful tools.

  

## Associated Projects and Initiatives

> [!important] Research on Labor Practices within DAOs
> 
>   
> We have a research paper coming out soon based on interviews of several DAO workers to understand their compensation and working conditions more deeply.  

  

# QUESTIONS!

---

## How do you plan to incorporate BREAD in your org?

> [!important] BREAD will be used for supporting worker organizing in unions and the DAO space as well as operational funding.

## What do you plan to do in the short term with the funds received from Breadchain?

> [!important] We’ll use BREAD to help us fund the completion of our research into labor practices in DAOs.

## ==What processes do you have to determine how funds from Breadchain are allocated?==

_==e.g., proposals, directly to contributors, etc.==_

> [!important] Breadchain funds contributed to LaborDAO for organizing will be managed by the core team and we’ll create a voting process for BREAD contributed to help decide which worker organizations receive funding and for what purposes. Examples would be: Strikes, organizing and legal fees.

## What have you done so far with the funds you've received?

> [!important] We haven’t used any funds from Breadchain yet! But we’ll keep you updated.
