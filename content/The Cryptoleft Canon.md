# What is the cryptoleft?

The term cryptoleftists was coined by Joshua Dávila during the creation of the ==**[The Blockchain Socialist](https://theblockchainsocialist.com/)**== platform and the name of the [original subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/cryptoleftists/) to create a space for those interested in the intersection of crypto and left politics. More specifically, the audience includes those with an interest in new forms of socio-economic collectivity using crypto mechanisms. The first project to be spun out of the community was ==**[[about-bread-cooperative.md]]**==, co-founded by Joshua and others in the community.
