Executive Summary
Brief overview of the cooperative's current position and strategic focus for this quarter
Bread Co-op will prioritize strengthening our brand presence as a trusted post-capitalist crypto institution by completing the Solidarity Fund rebrand, hosting weekly community events, and pursuing institutional grant opportunities. Additionally, we will begin expanding our Solidarity Primitives ecosystem by launching Stacks v1 and developing Safety Net smart contracts to serve the crypto solidarity economy, tactically taking advantage of grant opportunities. We still want to establish ourselves in the Shared Security space by accelerating Gas Killer (GK) development toward v0.2 release by the end of the quarter and pursuing potential clients and grant opportunities to secure sustainable funding. For this quarter, since this is our second time going through this budgeting process, we hope to see payment proposals that have more measurable goals and clarity so that we can hold each other accountable for work done.

Payment Evaluation Criteria
Primary Evaluation Questions
Strategic Alignment: Does this work directly support one of our defined strategic priorities?
Resource Efficiency: Is the requested compensation reasonable for the expected impact?
Member Capability: Does the proposing member have demonstrated ability to execute this work?
Timeline Feasibility: Can this work be completed within the quarter with quality results?
Measurement: Are there clear, measurable outcomes defined?
All payment proposals must follow the proposal template.

Exclusions & Restrictions
Work Types NOT Eligible for Payment
Research unrelated to current directives
Work that conflicts with cooperative values
Duplicate efforts without clear differentiation
Personal projects without cooperative benefit
New large projects without a clear funding path
Payment Restrictions
No member may receive more than 5 hours of pay if they are working full-time somewhere else already
The strategic committee may recommend that payment to someone be ceased if they are not performing in a way that is conducive to the cooperative, ideally with input from the project owner
Retroactive payment requests older than 3 months will not be considered, no past volunteering or contributions may be redeemed for payment in the future under any conditions.
